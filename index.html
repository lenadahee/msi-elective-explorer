Cross-Track electives

These courses show up in multiple pathways' top picks — taking courses outside your track is common and encouraged.

SI 564 — SQL & Databases

Taken as an elective across 3 of 4 tracks — BDA (~70%), UCAD (~50%), UX (~10%).

SI 511 — Special Topics

Appears in all four tracks — students use it to explore niche areas each term.

A technical course that appears even in humanities tracks — UX (~18%), LAKES (~30%).

SI 631 — Agile Software Development

SI 559 — Intro to AR/VR

Popular across interactive and humanities tracks — UX, UCAD, and LAKES.
